## What's new on Cloomba

A running log of significant updates and new features.

---

### October 2026 — One event page, and Manage in one menu

On the website:

**Event page**
- **One page** — the description, then the Program (or the dates of a series), Speakers, Photos and Sponsors, each with a link to the full list ([program and speakers](/help/agenda-and-speakers))
- **Photos** — guests add photos and videos with **Add photos**, right on the event page ([event photos](/help/media-wall))
- **Chat** — the event's chat opens from a button in the corner of the page ([event chat](/help/event-chat))
- **Staff buttons** — **Post**, **Guests**, **Invite** and **Manage** right under the date; from an hour before the start, **Check in** takes Invite's place ([check-in](/help/check-in))
- **Ended** and **Cancelled** badges on the date

**Manage**
- **One menu** — Overview, Registration, Guests, Invitations, Check in, Program, Speakers, Sponsors, Polls, Reports and Advanced, beside the page on a wide screen and in a dropdown on a phone
- **Invitations and Check in** sit in Manage, next to the guest list ([inviting guests](/help/invite-guests))
- **Organizer guides** — every guide, one click away in the menu

### October 2026 — Personal and team calendars, payments, and refunds

**Calendars**
- **Your personal calendar** — every account has one: a private home for your own events, with their guests on its People tab and its own payment accounts. Convert it to a team calendar when it should have a public page ([what calendars are](/help/about-calendars))
- **Several owners** — a calendar can have more than one owner, all equal; make an admin an owner to hand a calendar over ([owners and admins](/help/calendar-admins))
- **Payment accounts on the calendar** — each calendar connects its own Stripe accounts, and every event is paid into one of its calendar's accounts ([how it works](/help/payment-accounts))
- **Email allowances per calendar** — invitations, updates and newsletters count towards the calendar they belong to ([sending limits](/help/email-sending-limits))
- **API keys and webhooks on the calendar** — made by its owners on its Settings tab, reaching the calendar's events
- **Change calendar** — move an event to another calendar from its Overview, and see what changes before you confirm ([how it works](/help/move-event-to-calendar))

**Tickets & payments**
- **Cancelling and refunding are separate** — a guest's cancel frees their place and never refunds; you refund from the guest list, also after a cancel, and the cancel-event dialog has a **Refund** box ([issuing refunds](/help/refunds))
- **Your refund policy** — write it once on the calendar's Payments tab, and it shows below the description of its paid events

**Events**
- **One field for the place** — type a venue, or paste a meeting link for an online event ([event location](/help/event-location))
- **Register without an account** — on the website, guests join with their name and email ([registering](/help/rsvp-to-event))

### September 2026 — Memberships, payment accounts, and Telegram

**Calendars**
- **Memberships** — offer free or paid tiers on your calendar, paid once or renewing monthly or yearly, with member-only events and tickets; approve each member yourself, or let people with an email on your domain join right away
- **Newsletters to groups** — send a [calendar newsletter](/help/calendar-newsletters) to everyone or only to a membership tier or a tag, then follow up with the people you missed
- **Tags on people** — tag the people on your calendar's People tab and filter the list by tag
- **Events from elsewhere** — paste the link to an event hosted on another site, and it appears on your calendar with a button to the original
- **Handing over a calendar** — an admin can leave a calendar, and the owner can hand it to one of its admins

**Tickets & payments**
- **Approve, then pay** — with [approval](/help/guest-approval) on a paid event, guests pay only after you approve them, within the time you set
- **Several payment accounts** — connect more than one Stripe account and choose which one each event is paid into ([how it works](/help/payment-accounts))
- **Check-in staff** — let someone check in guests at the door during the hours you set, without access to manage the event

**Events**
- **Materials** — upload slides, recordings, and documents to an event or to one of its speakers; registered guests find them on the About tab
- **Short links** — get a cloo.mba link for an event from its Share menu

**Telegram**
- **Sign in through @cloomba_bot** — press Start in the bot and you're signed in
- **Telegram links** — share an event with a Telegram link from its Share menu, and it opens right inside Telegram

**Apps**
- **iOS and Android 2.6** — join and pay for memberships in the app, see the calendars you run, belong to, and follow on one Calendars screen, and get asked about notifications when you first need them — joining an event, sending a message — instead of at sign-in

### August 2026 — Pricing options, calendars, and embeds

- **Pay what you want** — set a minimum (or zero) and let people choose what they pay
- **Price on request** — list a ticket type without publishing a number when the price depends on the details
- **One event, several languages** — write the description with a section per language; each visitor sees theirs
- **Public calendar directory** — [/calendars](/calendars) now lists public community calendars
- **Subscribe to a calendar** — iCal and RSS feeds; paste the link into Apple Calendar, Outlook, or Google and new events arrive on their own
- **Embeds** — put a calendar or a single event on your own site
- **Public API** — see [the developer docs](/developers/api)
