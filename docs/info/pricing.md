Cloomba has one rule: **free events are free**. No listing fees, no hidden charges, no subscription required to get started.

---

### Free events

If your event is free to attend, Cloomba costs you nothing. Create as many free events as you like, with unlimited attendees.

---

### Paid events

When you sell tickets, Cloomba charges a fee that is deducted from your payout — attendees always pay exactly the price you set.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Cloomba fee** | 5% of the ticket price |
| **Payment processing** | Standard payment processing (rate depends on the card and country) |
| **Payout currency** | Matches your Stripe account currency |

The exact breakdown for your specific event setup is shown to you on the ticket type editor before you publish — no surprises.

---

### Payouts

Payouts are handled by **Stripe**. Once your event has taken place, funds are transferred to your connected bank account on Stripe's standard payout schedule (typically 2–7 business days depending on your country and account).

To receive payouts you need to connect a Stripe account. You can do this from your event settings when you create your first paid ticket type.

---

### Refunds

If you issue a refund to an attendee, the ticket price is returned to them in full. Cloomba refunds its 5% fee too — unlike most ticketing platforms, you don't pay to refund. Whether the payment processing fee is returned depends on your Stripe settings.

---

### Do I need a subscription?

No. Running events on Cloomba does not require a paid plan, and free events cost nothing at any scale.

The free plan does have fair-use limits — on photos and videos per event, emails per month, team seats, and calendars — set so that a normal event never reaches them. A **Pro** plan lifts those limits and adds full event analytics and API access, for communities and businesses running events at scale.

Pro is not on sale yet and has no price: while we finish it, we enable it by hand for organisers we work with. [Get in touch](/info/contact) if you think you need it.
