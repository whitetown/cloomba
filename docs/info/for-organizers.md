Run your events on a platform that's actually European.

Registration, tickets, and check-in — everything you need to run a community event. EU-hosted, GDPR by design, and in your language.

---

### Free events stay free, forever

If your event is free to attend, Cloomba is free to use. No listing fee, no per-RSVP charge, no subscription required. The full feature set, for as many free events as you want.

For paid events, a flat **5% platform fee + standard payment processing** — both deducted from your payout. Attendees pay exactly the price you set. See [Pricing & Fees](/info/pricing).

---

### Everything you actually need

Registration with waitlists and approval, paid tickets, check-in at the door, calendars and recurring events, updates, chat, a photo wall, and your guest list as a CSV whenever you want it.

The full list, section by section: [Features](/info/features).

### Built in the EU

Servers in **Amsterdam**. Your data and your attendees' data stay in the EU. Every feature ships with export and delete built in.

### Independent, not VC-backed

Cloomba is run by **WhiteTown s.r.o.** in Bratislava. We grow when you grow. No engagement metrics to juice, no ads to sell, no algorithm to fight.
