Luma is the platform Cloomba is most often compared to — and the one we respect the most. They've built a clean, well-designed event tool. We didn't set out to copy Luma. We set out to build the European-hosted, independently-operated equivalent, with a few deliberate differences on top.

## What both do well

- Clean event pages with cover, description, RSVP, waitlist
- Free events, free; paid events through Stripe Connect
- Communities (Luma: calendars) that group an organizer's events
- Mobile-friendly web; passwordless sign-in

If your needs map cleanly to "a polished, modern event tool", you'd be happy on either.

## When Cloomba is the better fit

- **3% on paid tickets** — Free events are free, and paid tickets cost 3% plus card processing — no plan to buy for it. See [Pricing & Fees](/info/pricing).
- **Import in one click** — Paste a link to a Luma event and it arrives as a draft — cover, description, date, place, and the agenda if there is one. For a whole calendar, [send it to us](/switch/luma) and we move the events across for you, free.
- **Everyone's photos, in one place** — Every event ships [shared photos](/help/event-photos) — photo and video uploads, captions, [comments and reactions](/help/comments-and-reactions), real-time updates, and an [option to make them public](/help/make-photos-public) with explicit consent. It's part of every event.
- **EU data, GDPR by design** — Cloomba's servers run in Amsterdam (EU-West) — all event content, RSVPs, photos, and messages are stored on servers in the EU, while Luma is US-hosted. The [privacy policy](/legal/privacy) is short because the product collects less: no advertising profiles, analytics that count visits without cookies, and [photo consent](/help/media-consent) asked before anyone's first upload to an event.
- **Not English-first** — Cloomba's interface and the emails it sends are fully localized, and each person sees them in the language they picked, not the one you organize in. You can also write the [description in several languages](/help/multilingual-descriptions). See [Language and locale settings](/help/language-and-locale).
- **Talks and a call for papers** — An [agenda with speakers](/help/agenda-and-speakers) and sponsors on the event page, the current session on each guest's phone while the event runs, and a [call for papers](/help/call-for-papers) to collect the talks.
- **Payment your way** — Cloomba supports Stripe **or** an [off-platform payment mode](/help/off-platform-payment) (bank transfer, PayPal, cash) where Cloomba stores no money. Each event in a community can be paid into its own [payment account](/help/payment-accounts).
- **Telegram, built in** — Guests sign in with Telegram and can get their reminders there. @cloomba_bot lets them register without leaving Telegram.
- **Built for developer communities** — GitHub sign-in is a first-class option on [Cloomba's sign-in page](/help/sign-in), and a public API, [embeds](/help/embed-calendar) and [calendar feeds](/help/add-to-calendar) put your events on your own site.
- **An independent product** — Cloomba is run by [WhiteTown s.r.o.](/info/about), a small Slovak company. No VC pressure, no engagement-metric optimization, no roadmap rewrites because an investor needs a growth story.

## When Luma is the better fit

**Your guests find you by browsing Luma.** It has a large audience looking at events across calendars, and if that is where your people come from, staying can be simpler.
