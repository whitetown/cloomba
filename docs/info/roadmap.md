## Product roadmap

Cloomba is actively developed. Here's what we've shipped recently, what we're building next, and what's on the horizon.

## Recently shipped

Since launch we've added the most-requested organiser and attendee features:

- **Multiple ticket types per event** — early-bird, standard, VIP, and group tiers, each with its own price and limit
- **Granular notification preferences** — choose exactly which updates reach you by email, push, and Telegram
- **Follow organisers and topics** — follow an organiser, calendar, category, or city and get notified about new events
- **Organiser analytics** — attendance trends, conversion funnels, and revenue summaries for each event
- **Import events from other platforms** — bring an existing event over in a few clicks
- **Sign in with Telegram** — plus @cloomba_bot for one-tap walk-in RSVPs at the door
- **Improved discovery** — location-based browsing, categories, and curated picks

## Next

We're focused on stability, feedback, and a few high-value additions:

- **Follow individual events** — get notified about updates to a specific event, not just its organiser
- **Minimum-attendees threshold** — "this event happens only if N people sign up", with paid events left uncharged until it's confirmed
- **Richer messaging** — multiple photos and videos in a single message

## Later

- Group chats
- Richer organiser reporting and exports
- More payment and payout options across Europe

## How we prioritise

We build what organisers and attendees ask for most. If something you need is missing, [tell us](/info/contact) — it genuinely influences what we build next.
