Everything Cloomba does, in one list. Every item here is live today — nothing planned, nothing in beta. Free events are free on all of it; paid tickets carry a 5% platform fee plus payment processing, see [Pricing & Fees](/info/pricing).

## Event page

- **Your own page** — A public page with its own link, cover and description. [Unlisted and private events](/help/event-visibility) stay off the listings.
- **Cover and theme** — Upload a [cover](/help/event-cover-image) or pick a preset; the page takes its [colors](/help/event-themes) from the image.
- **Description** — [Markdown](/help/markdown) with images inline, and a [version in each language](/help/multilingual-descriptions) you write.
- **Venue and map** — An address with a map, or an [online event](/help/online-events) whose link is shown to registered guests only.
- **Found by city and category** — Events are listed [by city](/help/browse-by-city) and by [category and hashtag](/help/hashtags-and-categories), so people nearby find them without a link.
- **In the guest's language** — The page, the emails and the apps follow [each visitor's language](/help/language-and-locale).

## Registration

- **Register in one tap** — Guests [sign in](/help/sign-in) with Google, Apple, GitHub, email or Telegram and [register](/help/rsvp-to-event). No passwords.
- **Capacity and deadline** — Set a limit and a [closing time](/help/registration-deadline); registration closes itself.
- **Waitlist** — When the event is full, guests join the [waitlist](/help/waitlist) and move up as spots open.
- **Approval** — Review each request and [approve or decline](/help/guest-approval) before a spot is confirmed.
- **Questions** — Ask what you need at registration — text, numbers, choices, an email address. See [guest questions](/help/guest-questions).
- **Invitations** — [Invite](/help/invite-guests) by link or by email. The invitation list shows who accepted.

## Tickets

- **Ticket types** — Several [tiers](/help/ticket-types) per event, each with its own price, quantity and sale window.
- **Coupons** — Percentage or fixed-amount [discounts](/help/coupons), with a use limit.
- **Paid on Stripe** — Guests pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. The money goes to [your Stripe account](/help/payouts), not through ours.
- **Off-platform payment** — Bank transfer, PayPal or cash — you [mark tickets as paid](/help/off-platform-payment) yourself.
- **Refunds** — [Refund](/help/refunds) from the guest list. Our fee is refunded with it.
- **Wallet passes** — Tickets go into Apple Wallet and Google Wallet, with the QR code ready at the door.

## At the door

- **Scan the QR code** — Every ticket has a QR code; [scan it](/help/check-in) and the guest is in.
- **By name** — No code? Search the list and check the guest in by hand.
- **Hosts and moderators** — Share the door and the event with [co-hosts](/help/cohosts); moderators look after the wall and the chat.
- **Live count** — Checked in versus expected, updating as people arrive.

## Reaching your guests

- **Updates** — Post an [update](/help/event-updates) and every registered guest is notified.
- **Reminders** — Automatic [reminders](/help/notifications-overview) before the event; you choose which ones go out.
- **Email, push and Telegram** — Guests pick the channel — [email](/help/email-notifications), [push](/help/push-notifications) or Telegram. You never manage addresses.
- **Your own wording** — Edit the [approval and decline emails](/help/email-templates) sent in your name.

## Chat

- **Event chat** — Guests and organizers in [one thread](/help/event-chat), before, during and after the event.
- **Polls** — Ask the room; results update live.
- **Photos in chat** — Share a picture in the thread without posting it to the wall.
- **Reactions** — React to a message instead of writing "+1".
- **Direct messages** — [Message](/help/messaging) a guest or an organizer one to one.

## Photo wall

- **Photos and videos** — Guests [upload](/help/upload-photos) from their phone; everyone at the event sees the [wall](/help/media-wall) fill up.
- **Reactions and comments** — [Comments and reactions](/help/comments-and-reactions) on every photo, updating live.
- **Consent** — Uploaders [confirm consent](/help/media-consent); you decide whether the wall is [public](/help/open-media-wall) or for guests only.
- **Moderation** — Remove anything, [block a guest](/help/blocking-guests), [report abuse](/help/reporting-abuse).

## Calendars and recurring events

- **A calendar for your group** — All your events on one page people [follow](/help/calendar-subscriptions). See [calendars](/help/about-calendars).
- **Recurring events** — Weekly, monthly or custom [series](/help/recurring-events); each occurrence has its own guest list.
- **Submissions** — Other organizers [submit events](/help/submit-event-to-calendar) to your calendar; you approve.
- **Members** — Add co-organizers to the calendar; they can do everything you can.
- **Newsletter** — Email everyone the calendar has reached — followers, past guests, and contacts you add. Every email carries an unsubscribe link. See [writing to your followers](/help/about-calendars).
- **Calendar feeds** — An .ics feed for the calendar and for every event — [subscribe](/help/add-to-calendar) from Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook.
- **Clone an event** — [Copy](/help/clone-event) last month's event and change the date.

## Agenda and speakers

- **Sessions** — Talks with a time and a room, shown as a [schedule](/help/agenda-and-speakers) on the event page.
- **Speakers and sponsors** — Profiles with photo and bio, linked to their sessions.
- **Materials** — Speakers attach slides and files to their session.
- **Live agenda** — The current session on the guest's phone while the event runs.

## Apps and integrations

- **iOS and Android apps** — The whole product on the phone, for guests and organizers, with push notifications. See [mobile apps](/info/mobile-apps).
- **Telegram bot** — @cloomba_bot: sign in, register and get reminders without leaving Telegram.
- **Embed** — Put an [event](/help/embed-event) or a [calendar](/help/embed-calendar) on your own site with one snippet.
- **Short links and QR codes** — Every event gets a short link and a QR code for posters and slides.
- **API** — A public API for your events and calendars.

## Your data

- **Import** — Bring an event over from Meetup, Luma or Eventbrite, or paste any event URL.
- **Guest list export** — Download the [guest list](/help/guest-list-export) as CSV — status, check-in, and the answers to your questions.
- **Everything you own** — [Export](/help/data-export) the data your account holds, any time.
- **Hand over an event** — [Transfer](/help/transfer-event) an event to another organizer when you step down.
- **Delete** — Delete an event or [your account](/help/delete-account); the data [goes with it](/help/what-gets-deleted).
- **Hosted in the EU** — Database and files in the EU. No ad tech, no tracking pixels. See [privacy](/help/privacy-overview).
- **GDPR** — A [DPA for organizers](/legal/dpa-for-organizers), a public subprocessor list, and [your rights](/help/gdpr-rights) in plain language.
