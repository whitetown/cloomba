Meetup invented the model of recurring local groups around shared interests. Two decades on, the price has gone up, and a lot of organizers are looking around. Cloomba is built for the same thing — a group that meets again and again — and the group costs nothing.

---

## What both do well

- Recurring local groups / channels that build a following
- Public event pages with RSVPs
- Member discovery within the group
- Free for attendees to join

---

## When Cloomba is the better fit

**You don't want a monthly fee for your group.** A Cloomba [community](/help/about-communities) — the equivalent of a Meetup group — is free, and so is every free event in it. Several owners can run it together, with [admins](/help/community-admins) to help.

**You want to bring your group over without retyping it.** Paste a link to a Meetup event and it arrives as a draft — cover, description, date and place. For a whole group, [send it to us](/switch/meetup) and we move every event across for you, at no cost.

**You want to keep your guest list.** Download it any time as a [CSV](/help/guest-list-export) — names, answers to your [questions](/help/guest-questions), who checked in. People [follow your community](/help/follow-community) and hear about each new date, and a [newsletter](/help/community-newsletters) reaches your followers and past guests.

**You sell tickets.** Stripe with several [ticket types](/help/ticket-types), [coupons](/help/coupons) and [refunds](/help/refunds) from the guest list, paid into your own Stripe account — each event can use its own [payment account](/help/payment-accounts). Or take bank transfer, PayPal or cash with [off-platform payment](/help/off-platform-payment).

**You want more than a listing.** Guests share [photos and videos](/help/event-photos), [comment and react](/help/comments-and-reactions), talk in the [event chat](/help/event-chat) with polls, and get your [updates](/help/event-updates). A bigger event gets an [agenda with speakers](/help/agenda-and-speakers) and a [call for papers](/help/call-for-papers).

**You want control over who comes.** Set a capacity and a [deadline](/help/registration-deadline), run a [waitlist](/help/waitlist), [approve each request](/help/guest-approval), and [check guests in](/help/check-in) by the QR code on their ticket in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.

**Your guests speak different languages.** The pages, the emails and the apps follow [each guest's language](/help/language-and-locale), and you can write the [description in several languages](/help/multilingual-descriptions).

**Your guests don't want another password.** They [sign in](/help/sign-in) with Google, Apple, GitHub, email or Telegram, and get reminders by email, push or Telegram — whichever they pick.

**You have your own website.** [Embed](/help/embed-calendar) your community's calendar or a [single event](/help/embed-event), give people an [.ics feed](/help/add-to-calendar) for their calendar app, or use the public API.

**You want no ads, and your data in the EU.** Cloomba shows no ads, on the web or in the [iOS and Android apps](/info/mobile-apps). Its servers are in Amsterdam, and organizers get a [DPA](/legal/dpa-for-organizers). Meetup is hosted in the US.

## When Meetup is the better fit

**Your members find you by browsing Meetup.** It has a large audience looking for groups by topic and city, and if that is where your people come from, it can be worth the subscription.

| | Cloomba | Meetup |
|---|---|---|
| Hosting your group / community | Free | Monthly subscription (rate varies by region and plan) |
| Free events | Free | Included with the subscription |
| Paid events | 3% + standard payment processing | Subscription + fees + payment processing |
| Member limits | None | Tiered by subscription |
| Hosting | EU (Amsterdam) | US |
| Third-party tracking | None | Meta pixel and Google Tag Manager |

Meetup's exact pricing depends on your country and tier — check their current pricing page.
