## Not receiving emails?

If Cloomba emails aren't arriving, here are the most common causes and fixes.

## Check your spam folder

Look for emails from `no-reply@cloomba.com`. If you find them there, mark them as "Not spam" — your email client will remember for future messages.

## Check the Promotions tab

Gmail and some other email clients automatically sort event-related emails into a Promotions or Updates tab. Check those folders and move a Cloomba email to your Primary inbox to train the filter.

## Correct email address

Make sure your Cloomba account uses the email address you're checking. Open your profile settings to confirm which address is on file.

## Magic link emails

Magic links expire quickly. If you requested one and it hasn't arrived after a minute, go back to the sign-in page and request a fresh one. Don't click an old link — request a new one each time.

## Corporate or university email

Some organisations block external email or filter aggressively. Try signing in with a personal email address or use Google/Apple sign-in instead.

## Still not arriving?

[contact us](/info/contact) with your email address and a description of which emails are missing. We can check delivery logs on our end.
