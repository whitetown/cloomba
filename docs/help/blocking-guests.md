### Cancelling a guest's registration

To remove someone from your guest list, open **Manage › Guests**, open the menu on their row, and select **Cancel registration**. They are removed from the guest list and stop receiving event updates.

For paid events, cancelling a registration does not issue a refund on its own — to give the money back, select **Refund** in the same menu. See [Issuing refunds](/help/refunds).

---

### Keeping someone from joining again

Turn on **Require approval to join** in **Manage › Registration**. Every new registration then waits for you in **Manage › Guests**, under **Requests**, and you choose **Approve** or **Reject**. Reject the person's request and they stay off the guest list; if they ask again, the new request waits for you too. See [Manual guest approval](/help/guest-approval).

---

### Who can do this

The organizer and admins can cancel registrations and approve or reject requests. Only the organizer can issue refunds. Moderators see the guest list and run check-in, but cannot remove or reject anyone.

---

### Reporting abuse

If a guest's behavior goes beyond what you can handle as an organizer — harassment, threats, or illegal content — use **Report** on their message or photo, or contact us at [support (at) cloomba.com](/info/contact). We review all reports within 2 business days. See [Reporting abuse](/help/reporting-abuse).
