One field holds where your event happens: **Location or link**. Type a venue or an address for an in-person event, or paste a meeting link for an online one.

---

### In-person events

Start typing the venue or street address and pick it from the suggestions. Cloomba shows a map on your event page and uses the location for city-based discovery — people browsing events in your area can find the event by location.

When you open the field, it lists your recent locations — the venues of your latest events — so you can pick a place you have used before.

If the place you want is not among the suggestions, pick the last row, **Use “…”**, to keep the name exactly as you typed it.

---

### Online events

Paste the URL of your meeting room — Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, YouTube Live, or any other link — and choose **Online**. The event becomes an online event, and the link takes the place of the address. The URL is shown only to people who have registered, so it stays out of the public event page.

---

### Changing the location

You can update the location at any time after publishing — pick a new venue, or paste a link to move the event online. If the venue changes after people have already registered, use an event update to notify them directly.
