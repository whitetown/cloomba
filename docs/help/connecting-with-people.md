Cloomba has no search box for finding people. You connect with people you actually cross paths with — at an event, through a shared profile link, or face to face. It keeps conversations tied to real interactions instead of cold, unsolicited messages.

### Three ways to start a chat

**From an event.** Tap someone's name or photo on an event — an organiser, a speaker, or a fellow guest — to open their profile, then tap **Connect**.

**From a profile.** Every profile at `cloomba.com/u/username` has a **Connect** button. Once you already have a conversation, the same button reads **Chat** and takes you straight to it.

**By scanning their code.** When you're together in person, open your profile QR code — from your profile or **Messages** — and have the other person scan it with a phone camera. You're connected instantly, with no request to accept. One code can be scanned by several people, so it works for a whole table at once.

> You can display your own code on any device, including desktop web, but scanning someone else's needs a phone camera — there's no scanner built into the app.

### Message requests

The first message to someone you're not yet connected with arrives as a **request** the other person can accept or decline. Until it's accepted, that's the only message you can send. Scanning a code in person skips this step — being face to face is already the introduction.

### Why there's no people search

You can't look up strangers by name, and no one can drop you into a chat out of the blue. It's deliberate: it keeps spam and cold outreach off the platform, and it means a message almost always comes from someone you've genuinely met or share an event with.

### Blocking

If you'd rather not hear from someone, block them from their profile. A blocked person can no longer message you or see your activity.
