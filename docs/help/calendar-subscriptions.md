Following a calendar keeps you up to date with everything an organiser or community puts on.

---

### How to follow

Open any calendar page and click **Follow**. You will be notified when the calendar owner adds new events.

You need a Cloomba account to follow a calendar.

---

### What you get

- Notifications when new events are added to the calendar
- Easy access to the calendar's upcoming events from your account

Following a calendar does not automatically RSVP you to any events — you still register for each one individually when you want to attend.

---

### Unfollowing

Open the calendar page and click **Unfollow** at any time. You will stop receiving notifications from that calendar immediately.

---

### Finding calendars to follow

Browse calendars from the Discover page, or find them linked from event pages. When you attend an event that belongs to a calendar, you will see the calendar listed on the event page — a good way to discover series you might want to follow.
