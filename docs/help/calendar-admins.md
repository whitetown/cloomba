A calendar has one or more owners and any number of admins. Owners run the calendar together; admins help run it.

---

### What admins can do

Admins have the owner's trust. They can:

- Edit the calendar and add events to it
- Review submissions from other organizers
- See the **People** tab and write [newsletters](/help/calendar-newsletters)
- Invite the calendar's people to its events — see [Inviting guests](/help/invite-guests)
- See the calendar's [payment accounts](/help/payment-accounts) and choose one of them for an event

Only owners can add and remove people, change their roles, manage the calendar's payment accounts, API keys and webhooks, and delete the calendar. Newsletters an admin sends count towards the calendar's [email sending limits](/help/email-sending-limits), and membership payments go to the calendar's default [payment account](/help/payment-accounts).

---

### Adding someone

On the calendar's **Settings** tab, under **Admins**, select **Add**, enter the person's Cloomba username, and pick their **Role**: **Admin** or **Owner**. They are notified right away. The person needs a Cloomba account.

---

### Owners

Owners are equal. Each owner can add and remove people, owners included, and change anyone's role: pick **Owner** or **Admin** in their row under **Admins**. You step down from owner to admin the same way, in your own row. The person whose role changes is notified.

A calendar always keeps at least one owner: the last owner cannot step down, be removed, or leave.

---

### Admins and the calendar's events

When an event is put on a calendar as its home, the calendar's owners and admins become admins of that event too: they can edit it and manage its guests, and their names appear under **Hosted by**. See [Adding co-hosts and managers](/help/cohosts) for what an event admin can do.

This access comes with the calendar and ends with it — when the person stops being an admin of the calendar, when the event moves off the calendar, or when the calendar is deleted. Access someone was given by hand on the event itself stays.

Someone who becomes an admin later does not get access to events that were already on the calendar. An event's organizer can also remove any of them from that one event.

---

### Removing someone

An owner removes an admin or another owner from the **Admins** list. If the person organizes upcoming events on the calendar, the dialog offers **Take their upcoming events off this calendar**. Leave it unticked and those events stay on the calendar; tick it and they leave. Past events always stay.

Either way, the person loses access to the calendar and to its other events, and is notified. Guests of events taken off the calendar stop counting on the **People** tab.

---

### Leaving a calendar

An admin, or an owner while another owner stays, can leave from the **Danger zone** at the bottom of the **Settings** tab: select **Leave calendar**. If you organize upcoming events on the calendar, you can tick **Move my upcoming events to my personal calendar**; otherwise they stay. The owners are notified.

---

### Handing a calendar to someone else

Make them an owner — add them with the **Owner** role, or pick **Owner** in their row if they are already an admin. Then step down to admin in your own row, or leave the calendar.

The calendar's address, events, followers, members, and payment accounts stay as they are. Every event keeps its organizer. To hand over an event, see [Transferring event ownership](/help/transfer-event).

---

### Deleting a calendar

An owner deletes the calendar from the **Danger zone** with **Delete**, once they are its only owner — the other owners leave or are removed first. Its events are kept: an event whose home it was moves to its organizer's personal calendar, and an event that was only listed on it stops being listed. The admins lose the access to those events that came with the calendar.

A calendar cannot be deleted while any of its membership tiers has a price, or while an upcoming event on it has members-only tickets.
