### How to create one

Sign in and open **Calendars** in the top bar, then select **New calendar**. Give it a name — that is all it asks for. You land on the calendar's manage page, where everything else is set up.

You can create a calendar from the mobile apps as well. Editing a calendar stays on the web.

---

### Setting it up

The **Settings** tab is what you land on. Everything on it saves as you go:

- **Name** and **description** — what kind of events to expect and who the calendar is for
- **Logo** (square) and **banner** (wide) — the banner becomes the header of the calendar page
- **Address** — the field under the name, `cloomba.com/c/…`. The calendar starts with a generated one; type a readable one and select **Save**, and the page moves to the new URL
- **Location** — the city the calendar is based in; it links to that city's page
- **Let others submit events** — off by default; see [Submitting an event to a calendar](/help/submit-event-to-calendar)
- **Links** — your website and social profiles
- **People & sponsors** — organizers, speakers, and partners shown on the calendar page
- **Admins** — people who can edit the calendar, add events, review submissions, and write to your audience; see [Calendar admins and ownership](/help/calendar-admins)

Calendars are always public — anyone can open the page and follow it. Public events are shown to everyone. An unlisted event shows only to the calendar's members, when it offers memberships, and private events never appear.

---

### Adding events

- **When you create an event** — the calendar you pick above the title becomes its home calendar. New events start on your default calendar; star a calendar on your Calendars page to make it the default. To put an existing event on the calendar, select **Change calendar** on the **Calendar** card at the bottom of the event's **Overview** — see [Moving an event to another calendar](/help/move-event-to-calendar).
- **From the calendar page** — **Submit Event** offers owners and admins three options: create a new event on the calendar, add an existing Cloomba event (yours, or any public event by link), or add an external event that happens outside Cloomba. Your additions go live immediately; they never wait in a queue.
- **From other organizers** — turn on **Let others submit events** and review what comes in. See [Submitting an event to a calendar](/help/submit-event-to-calendar).

### The manage page

Open **Manage** on the calendar page, or the pencil on your Calendars page. The tabs:

- **Events** — every event on the calendar, with the submissions queue behind a button on the toolbar
- **People** — everyone the calendar reaches: followers, guests of every event whose home is this calendar (whoever organized it), and contacts you import; tag them, export them, and manage memberships
- **Newsletters** — write to those people; see [Calendar newsletters](/help/calendar-newsletters)
- **Payments** — the calendar's [payment accounts](/help/payment-accounts) and its discount codes
- **Reports** — traffic and guests of the calendar's events, summed
- **Settings** — everything above, plus the embed snippet and the **Danger zone**: **Leave calendar** for admins (and for an owner while another owner stays), **Delete** for owners

Deleting a calendar keeps its events: an event whose home it was moves to its organizer's personal calendar, and an event that was only listed on it stops being listed. A calendar can't be deleted while any of its membership tiers has a price, or while an upcoming event on it has members-only tickets. See [Calendar admins and ownership](/help/calendar-admins).
