Cloomba organizes in-person events by city, so you can see what's happening in a place you live or are visiting.

---

### City pages

Every supported city has its own page at `/city/<slug>` — for example `/city/bratislava` or `/city/prague`. The page lists upcoming public events anchored to that city.

When you create an in-person event, the venue address is automatically matched to the nearest city in our curated list (within ~65 km). You don't set the city manually — give a good address and the right city follows.

---

### The Explore Local block

On the [Discover page](/help/find-events), the **Explore Local** block shows cities with public events coming up. Only cities with at least one upcoming event are listed, so the block stays useful.

---

### Events near you

Discover shows upcoming events in the country you're browsing from — **Events in Slovakia**, for example. Events in the cities you follow are listed first, so following a city brings its events to the top.

---

### Online events

Online events don't have a city — they appear under their own filter rather than on any city page. See [Hosting online events](/help/online-events).

---

### Subscribing to a city

On any city page you can hit **Follow** to subscribe. You'll be notified when new events are added in that city.
