The bell icon in the navigation bar is your notifications inbox. A red badge on the bell tells you how many unread notifications you have across everything — RSVPs, event updates, invitations, calendar follows, media-wall activity.

---

### Opening the inbox

Tap the bell to see the list. The most recent notifications are at the top.

Each notification:

- Shows what happened and which event or calendar it's about
- Has an icon hinting at the kind (calendar, person, photo, etc.)
- Is a deep link — tap it to land on the right page

---

### Marking things read

Opening the inbox marks visible notifications as read. The badge count updates instantly.

---

### Real-time

When something new happens, the badge ticks up without you needing to refresh — as long as your Cloomba tab is open or you have push notifications enabled.

---

### Push notifications

Push notifications are the same events, delivered to your device's notification center. See [Setting up push notifications](/help/push-notifications) to turn them on.

---

### Email notifications

Some kinds of notification — RSVP confirmations, event updates, invitations — are also sent by email. See [Email reminders & alerts](/help/email-notifications).

---

### Choosing where notifications reach you

The inbox lists every notification. To choose which kinds also reach you by email, push, or Telegram, open **Preferences** in your account on the web, or **Settings → Notifications** in the app. Each kind — event reminders, organizer announcements, registrations, comments and mentions, invitations, chat requests, calendar newsletters, and the daily digest — has its own switch for each channel. Account and payment alerts always arrive.
