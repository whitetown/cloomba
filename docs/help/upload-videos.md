Video uploads work like photo uploads but with a few extra considerations around file size and organiser permissions.

---

### Who can upload videos

Video uploads are **off by default**. Organisers must explicitly enable them in the event settings under **Allow attendee video uploads**. Once enabled, any confirmed attendee can upload a video.

Organisers and admins can always upload videos regardless of this setting.

---

### How to upload

Open the media wall and tap the upload button. Select a video file from your device. You can add a caption before posting.

---

### Supported formats and limits

Supported formats: **MP4** (`video/mp4`) and **MOV** (`video/quicktime`). File size limit: **500 MB** per video.

Keep clips short — a minute or two is ideal for event moments. Longer recordings are usually better hosted on YouTube or Vimeo and linked from the event description or an update.

---

### Captions

Add a caption to give context — who is speaking, what moment this is, or anything else worth noting. You can edit the caption at any time after posting.

---

### Deleting videos

You can delete your own video posts at any time. Organisers, admins, and moderators can delete any video on the wall.
