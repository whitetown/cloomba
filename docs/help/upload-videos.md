Video uploads work like photo uploads, with a few extra considerations around file size and organizer permissions.

---

### Who can upload videos

Guest video uploads are **off by default**. Organizers turn them on in **Manage › Overview**, in the **Options** card, with **Let guests upload videos** (in the app, the same switch is on the event's **Manage** screen). Once it is on, any confirmed guest can upload a video.

Staff — organizers, admins, and moderators — can always upload videos, whatever this setting says.

---

### How to upload

Open the event page, find the **Photos** section, and tap or click **Add photos** in its header. Select a video file from your device; it is added straight away.

---

### Supported formats and limits

Supported formats: **MP4** (`video/mp4`) and **MOV** (`video/quicktime`). File size limit: **500 MB** per video.

Each event can hold a set number of videos, depending on the organizer's plan.

Keep clips short — a minute or two is ideal for event moments. Longer recordings are usually better hosted on YouTube or Vimeo and linked from the event description or an update.

---

### Captions

Open a video you uploaded to add a caption — who is speaking, what moment this is, or anything else worth noting. You can change it at any time.

---

### Deleting videos

You can delete your own videos at any time. Organizers, admins, and moderators can delete any video.
