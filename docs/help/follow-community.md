Following a community keeps you up to date with everything it puts on.

---

### How to follow

Open the community page and select **Follow**. If you are not signed in, a sign-in prompt opens right there and the follow completes as soon as you are in.

You can also follow from an event page: when the event belongs to a community, the community is shown under **Presented by**, with a Follow button beside it. In the mobile apps, Follow is on the community page as well.

---

### What you get

- A notification when a new event is added to the community — sent once the organizer has finished setting it up, and only for events that are still ahead
- The community's upcoming events in **Following** in your account, together with everything else you follow
- The community on your Communities page
- Newsletters the organizer sends to followers

Following a community does not register you for any event — you still register for each one individually.

New-event notifications arrive as push notifications. In your notification settings you can switch to a daily digest — one message a day with every new event from what you follow — and turn newsletters from communities you follow off. Every newsletter also carries its own unsubscribe link. See [Notifications overview](/help/notifications-overview).

---

### Your own calendar app

Next to Follow, the feed menu subscribes your calendar app to the community: **Google Calendar**, **Apple Calendar / Outlook**, or any reader that takes an RSS feed. A subscription keeps itself up to date as events are added or changed — unlike a downloaded .ics file, which is a one-time snapshot.

---

### Memberships

Some communities offer memberships — free or paid tiers with members-only events and tickets. Joining one makes you a follower too. Cancelling a membership keeps you following the community; unfollowing does not cancel a membership.

---

### Unfollowing

Open the community page and select **Following** — it turns back to Follow, and the notifications from that community stop.

---

### Finding communities to follow

Browse the [communities directory](/communities), or the featured communities on Discover. An event that belongs to a community names it on its page — when you register for one event of a series, that is the quickest way to follow the rest of it.
