Push notifications deliver alerts directly to your device so you do not have to be in the app to stay informed.

---

### Enabling on the web

When you first use Cloomba, your browser may ask for permission to send notifications. Click **Allow** to enable them.

If you dismissed the prompt, you can enable notifications manually:

1. Open your account settings.
2. Go to **Notifications**.
3. Click **Enable push notifications** and allow the browser permission when prompted.

Push notifications on the web work only when you have Cloomba open in a browser tab, or when the browser is running in the background. For more reliable delivery, use the mobile app.

---

### Enabling on mobile

Open the Cloomba app and go to your account settings. Tap **Notifications** and enable push notifications. Your device may ask for permission — tap **Allow**.

---

### If notifications are not arriving

- Make sure you have allowed notifications in both Cloomba's settings and your device or browser settings.
- On iOS, check Settings → Cloomba → Notifications.
- On Android, check Settings → Apps → Cloomba → Notifications.
- In the browser, check the site permissions for cloomba.com.

If you have checked all of the above and notifications are still not working, contact us at [support@cloomba.com](/info/contact).

---

### Turning off push notifications

Go to your account settings, open **Notifications**, and disable push notifications. You can also revoke permission directly from your browser or device settings at any time.
