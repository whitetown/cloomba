## Can't sign in?

Cloomba uses passwordless sign-in — there's no password to reset. Here's how to fix the most common sign-in issues.

## Magic link not arriving

1. **Check your spam folder** — look for an email from `no-reply (at) cloomba.com`
2. **Check the Promotions tab** — Gmail sometimes routes these there
3. **Wait a moment** — delivery can take up to a minute
4. **Request a new link** — magic links expire after a few minutes; go back to the sign-in page and try again
5. **Try a different method** — if you signed up with Google or Apple, use that instead

## Signed in to a different account

A magic link, Google and GitHub find your account by its email address. You land in a new, empty account when you sign in with:

- an email address you have not used on Cloomba before
- Telegram, before you added it to your account
- Apple with **Hide My Email** turned on — Apple gives Cloomba a different address

If your events or RSVPs seem missing, sign out and sign in the way you did before. Then add the other method under **Sign-in methods** — on your **Profile** page on the web, or in your account in the app — so it opens the same account next time.

## Account seems to be gone

If you can't find your data after signing in, check that you're using the same email address — or the same Telegram account — you originally signed up with. If you're still stuck, [contact us](/info/contact) with your email address and we'll look it up.
