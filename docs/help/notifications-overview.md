Cloomba sends notifications to keep you informed about events you care about — without flooding you with noise.

---

### What triggers a notification

**As an attendee:**
- Your RSVP is confirmed or declined
- You move from the waitlist to confirmed
- The organiser posts an event update
- The event is cancelled or rescheduled
- A reminder before the event starts
- Someone reacts to or comments on your media post

**As an organiser:**
- Someone RSVPs to your event
- A guest requests approval (if Require approval is on)
- A submission arrives for your calendar
- Someone uploads to your event media wall

**As a calendar follower:**
- A new event is added to a calendar you follow

---

### How notifications are delivered

Cloomba delivers notifications in two ways:

**In-app** — notifications appear in your notification inbox inside Cloomba, accessible from the bell icon in the navigation.

**Push notifications** — if you have enabled push notifications in your browser or the mobile app, time-sensitive alerts are delivered directly to your device. See [Setting up push notifications](/help/push-notifications).

---

### Managing your preferences

You can control which notifications you receive from your account settings. Some notifications — like RSVP confirmations and event cancellations — are always sent as they are essential to using the platform.
