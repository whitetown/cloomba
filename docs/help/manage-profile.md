## Editing your profile

You can update your Cloomba profile at any time from your account settings.

## What you can change

- **Display name** — the name shown on guest lists and in comments
- **Username** — your @handle used for direct invitations
- **Bio** — a short description visible on your public profile
- **Profile photo** — your avatar shown across the platform

## How to edit

1. Open **Account** from the navigation
2. Tap **Profile**
3. Make your changes
4. Tap **Save**

## Changing your email address

Your email address is tied to your sign-in method. If you signed in with Google or Apple, the email is managed by that provider. To use a different email, sign out and sign back in with magic link using the new address — a new account will be created.

## Changing your sign-in method

Each sign-in method (Google, Apple, GitHub, magic link, phone) creates a separate account on Cloomba. You cannot merge accounts or switch providers on an existing account.
