Any organizer can submit their event to a calendar that accepts submissions. The calendar owner reviews the submission and approves or declines it — nothing appears on the calendar without their yes.

---

### Submitting an event

1. Open the calendar's page and select **Submit Event**.
2. The picker opens with your upcoming events already listed — type to search the rest of your events.
3. Pick up to 5 events and submit them in one step.

The calendar owner is notified of your submission.

If you manage the calendar yourself (owner or admin), your events skip the queue and go live on the calendar immediately.

---

### What happens next

The calendar owner reviews your submission from their calendar management page. They can:

- **Approve** — your event is added to the calendar and its followers are notified.
- **Decline** — your event is not added. You can submit it to a different calendar instead.

---

### For calendar owners — managing submissions

Incoming submissions appear in a queue on your calendar management page. Review each one and approve or decline. There is no deadline — submissions stay in the queue until you act on them.

Review is not optional: approving publishes the event to your calendar and notifies every follower, so it never happens without you saying yes.

You can remove an approved event from your calendar at any time, even after followers were notified.

---

### Accepting submissions

Submissions are off by default. To let other organizers submit to your calendar, turn on **Allow submissions** in the calendar's settings.
