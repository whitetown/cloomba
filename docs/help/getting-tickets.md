## Getting and finding your tickets

When you RSVP to a paid event on Cloomba, your ticket is tied to your account — there's no separate file to download or store.

## Buying a ticket

1. Open the event page and tap **Join**
2. Pick a ticket type if the event offers more than one
3. Complete checkout via Stripe — card payment is accepted
4. You'll receive a confirmation email once payment is processed

If the organiser is using **off-platform payment** instead of Stripe, you'll see the payment instructions on the registration screen — pay outside Cloomba and the organiser will confirm your spot manually.

## Tickets without a fixed price

**Pay what you want** — you choose the amount. What's shown is a minimum and checkout starts there; you can pay more. If the minimum is 0, leaving it alone gets you in for free with no card at all.

**On request** — the organiser hasn't published the price. You register first and settle it with them: either the amount appears at the payment step, or your place holds as pending until you agree one and they record it.

## If registration has closed

Some organisers stop taking sign-ups before the event starts. When that happens the page says **Registration has closed for this event** and there's no Join button. This isn't a full event, so there's no waitlist to join — but [ask the organiser](/help/contact-organizer): an invitation sent after the deadline still works.

## Finding your ticket

Your confirmed tickets are always accessible from your profile. Open **Account → My tickets** (or the event page itself) to see your booking.

## At the door

Show your ticket confirmation to the organiser at check-in. The organiser can scan your QR code or look up your name on their guest list.

## Something went wrong with payment

If your payment was declined or didn't complete, no ticket has been issued. Try again with a different card, or [contact us](/info/contact) with the event name and your email address.
