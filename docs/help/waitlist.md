The waitlist automatically queues guests when your event reaches capacity. You do not need to manage it manually — Cloomba handles the queue.

---

### How it works

Set a **capacity** on your event. Once that number of confirmed guests is reached, new registrations go to the waitlist instead of being confirmed.

When a confirmed guest cancels their RSVP, the first person on the waitlist whose party fits the freed space is automatically confirmed and notified — nothing to accept, their spot is simply theirs.

---

### Enabling the waitlist

The waitlist is active automatically whenever you set a capacity limit. There is no separate toggle — if your event has a cap and it is full, new registrations go to the waitlist.

Leave capacity blank if you do not want a limit and do not need a waitlist.

---

### Where the limit lives

The event's own **Capacity** field is the total for the whole event, and it always applies — no matter how many ticket types you add. Each ticket type can additionally carry its own **Limit capacity**, which caps that one type; a type without its own limit simply shares whatever remains of the event total.

So a capacity of 50 with an early-bird type limited to 10 means at most 50 guests overall, of which at most 10 arrive on the early-bird price. Set the event Capacity when you care about the room; add per-type limits when you also want to ration a specific type. See [Ticket types and how you price them](/help/ticket-types).

---

### A full event is not a closed event

The waitlist is for events that are **full**. It has nothing to do with a registration deadline: if you have stopped taking sign-ups because your [sales window](/help/registration-deadline) has passed, there is no waitlist and no queue — the event simply says registration has closed. Nobody is waiting for a spot, because no cancellation would release one.

There is one more case with no waitlist: a ticket type priced **on request** with no amount set holds each registration at Pending payment, and those holds occupy the capacity. Once they fill it, the type reads as sold out rather than opening a queue.

---

### What guests see

When an event is full, the RSVP button shows a **Join waitlist** option. Guests who join the waitlist can see their position in the queue. They receive a notification as soon as a spot becomes available.

---

### Managing the waitlist

You can see the full waitlist in your guest list under the **Waitlist** status. You cannot manually reorder the queue — spots are offered in the order guests joined.

If you increase the capacity of your event, guests at the top of the waitlist are automatically moved to confirmed.
