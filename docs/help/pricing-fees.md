### Free events

If your event is free to attend, Cloomba is completely free to use. No listing fee, no per-RSVP charge, no subscription.

---

### Paid events

When you collect ticket payments, Cloomba deducts fees from your payout. Attendees always pay exactly the price you set — nothing is added on top.

**Two fees apply:**

**Cloomba fee — 5%**
Our platform fee on each ticket sold.

**Standard payment processing**
Stripe's fee for handling the payment. The exact rate depends on the card type and country — your event editor shows the exact net payout for the price and currency you choose, before you publish.

---

### When do you get paid?

Payouts go to your connected Stripe bank account after the event. Stripe's standard schedule is **2–7 business days** depending on your country and account settings. You can view and manage your payout schedule directly in your Stripe dashboard.

---

### Refunds

If you refund a ticket, the attendee receives the full ticket price back. Cloomba refunds its 5% fee too — unlike most ticketing platforms, you don't pay to refund. Whether the payment processing fee is returned depends on your Stripe account settings.

---

### No monthly fees or tiers

There is no subscription, no Pro plan, and no feature gating. Every organiser on Cloomba gets the same platform. See [Pricing & Fees](/info/pricing) for the full overview.
