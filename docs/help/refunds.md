Refunds for paid tickets are processed through **Stripe**. Cloomba does not have a built-in refund button — you issue refunds directly from your Stripe dashboard.

---

### How to issue a refund

1. Log in to your [Stripe dashboard](https://dashboard.stripe.com).
2. Go to **Payments** and find the charge you want to refund.
3. Click **Refund** and confirm the amount.

Stripe processes the refund and returns the money to the guest's original payment method. Refunds typically appear within 5–10 business days depending on the guest's bank.

---

### What is refunded

The guest receives the full ticket price back, and **Cloomba refunds its 5% platform fee** too — unlike most ticketing platforms, you don't pay to refund. Stripe's payment-processing fee is kept by Stripe and isn't returned to you; that's standard across every Stripe-based platform, not a Cloomba choice.

---

### When you must refund

If you cancel a paid event, you are required to refund all attendees in full. Process refunds through your Stripe dashboard for each ticket sold.

---

### Refund policy on your event page

State your refund policy clearly in the event description — for example whether guests can request a refund if they cancel before a certain date. Where no policy is stated, guests are entitled to a full refund up to the start time of the event.
