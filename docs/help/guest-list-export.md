You can export your full guest list at any time — as CSV, Excel (XLSX), or a printable list — before, during, or after the event.

---

### What the export includes

The CSV contains one row per guest with:

- Name and username
- RSVP status (confirmed, waitlisted, cancelled, etc.)
- Registration date
- Check-in status and time
- Phone and email, where the guest provided them
- Plus-ones
- Ticket type and amount paid (for paid events)
- Answers to any registration questions you set up

---

### How to export

Open the **Guests** tab on your event page and choose **Export** — CSV, Excel (.xlsx), or Print view (HTML). The print view is the door list: print it, or save it as a PDF.

---

### When to use it

- **Before the event** — print a door list or import guests into another tool.
- **On the day** — a backup in case you need an offline attendance record.
- **After the event** — keep a record of who attended for reporting or follow-up.

---

### Data and privacy

The export contains personal data. Handle it in accordance with your GDPR obligations as a data controller — store it securely, use it only for the purpose it was collected, and delete it when you no longer need it.
