Your event page takes its colours from your cover image, automatically. There is no theme to choose — pick a cover you like and the page is styled to match it.

---

### How it works

When you set a cover image, Cloomba reads the colours out of it and builds a palette for your event: a background, a surface for cards, an accent for buttons, and matching text colours. It builds a light version and a dark version, so the page looks right whichever mode your guests browse in.

The whole event page adopts that palette — including the navigation bar and the footer — on the web and in the Cloomba mobile apps. Guests see the same colours everywhere.

---

### Changing the colours

To change your event's colours, **change the cover image**. That is the only lever, and it is the point: the page always agrees with the picture at the top of it.

You can upload your own image, or pick one from Cloomba's cover library. Every event gets a cover from the library when it is created, so your page is never unstyled.

See [Event cover image](/help/event-cover-image) for what makes a good cover.

---

### Previewing the palette

Open your event, go to **Edit**, and find the **Appearance** card. It shows the colours Cloomba pulled from your current cover. Swap the cover and the swatches update.

---

### Turning it off

If the cover colours are not what you want for a particular event, switch on **Use default app colors** in the Appearance card. The event is then shown in Cloomba's standard light and dark theme, and the cover palette is ignored — on the web and in the mobile apps.

Your palette is kept, so you can switch back at any time without re-uploading anything.
