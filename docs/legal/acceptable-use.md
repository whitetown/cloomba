This policy sets out what is and is not allowed on Cloomba. It applies to all users — organisers, attendees, and anyone who interacts with the platform.

Violations may result in content removal, account suspension, or permanent termination. Serious violations may be reported to law enforcement.

---

### Events and content

**Allowed:**
- Public and private events of any size — meetups, conferences, parties, workshops, performances, community gatherings.
- Paid and free events, with or without registration questions.
- Events with restricted attendance (invite-only, approval required, waitlisted).

**Not allowed:**
- Events that promote or facilitate illegal activity.
- Events that incite hatred, discrimination, or violence based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or any other protected characteristic.
- Events designed to defraud attendees — fake venues, misrepresented speakers, non-existent activities.
- Events that violate third-party intellectual property rights (e.g. unauthorised use of licensed music, film screenings without a licence).
- Adult content or events that sexualise minors in any way.

---

### Accounts and identity

**Not allowed:**
- Creating multiple accounts to evade a suspension or ban.
- Impersonating another person, organisation, or brand.
- Sharing your account with others or using someone else's account.
- Using automated tools to create accounts, scrape data, or interact with the platform at scale without written permission.

---

### Communications

**Not allowed:**
- Sending unsolicited commercial messages (spam) to attendees or other users.
- Harassing, threatening, or intimidating other users.
- Sharing another user's private information without their consent (doxxing).
- Using event updates or messages to distribute malware or phishing links.

---

### Payments

**Not allowed:**
- Collecting payments through Cloomba for events you do not intend to run.
- Misrepresenting ticket prices, fees, or refund policies.
- Attempting to circumvent Cloomba's payment processing or fees.
- Using the platform to launder money or conduct fraudulent transactions.

---

### Platform integrity

**Not allowed:**
- Attempting to access accounts, systems, or data you are not authorised to access.
- Probing, scanning, or testing the vulnerability of our systems without prior written authorisation.
- Interfering with the availability of the platform (e.g. denial-of-service attacks).
- Reverse engineering or copying Cloomba's software or design.

---

### Reporting violations

If you see content or behaviour that violates this policy, please report it using the report function on the relevant event or profile, or email us at [support (at) cloomba.com](/info/contact).

We review all reports and aim to respond within 2 business days.

---

### Enforcement

We may take any of the following actions depending on the severity and frequency of a violation:

- Remove the content.
- Issue a warning.
- Temporarily suspend the account.
- Permanently terminate the account.
- Withhold or reverse payouts where fraud is suspected.
- Report the matter to law enforcement or relevant authorities.
