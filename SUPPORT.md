# Support

Need a hand with Cloomba? Here's where to go.

| Your situation                                          | Best place                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ❓ A question about how to use Cloomba                  | [Discussions → Q&A](https://github.com/whitetown/cloomba/discussions/categories/q-a)                                                                                                  |
| 💡 An idea or improvement                               | [Discussions → Ideas](https://github.com/whitetown/cloomba/discussions/categories/ideas) or a [feature request](https://github.com/whitetown/cloomba/issues/new?template=feature.yml) |
| 🐞 Something is broken                                  | [Bug report](https://github.com/whitetown/cloomba/issues/new?template=bug.yml)                                                                                                        |
| 🔒 A security or privacy concern                        | [Report privately](https://github.com/whitetown/cloomba/security/advisories/new) — see [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md)                                                                   |
| 💳 Account, billing, refunds, or a privacy/GDPR request | [support@cloomba.com](mailto:support@cloomba.com)                                                                                                                                       |

For anything involving your personal data, payment details, or another person's information, please
email us rather than posting publicly.

We're a small team — community questions in Discussions are often answered fastest because other
organisers and attendees can help too. 💬
