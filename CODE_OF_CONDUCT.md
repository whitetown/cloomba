# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want the Cloomba community — issues, discussions, and everywhere else we interact — to be a
welcoming, friendly, and harassment-free place for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible
or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Please:

- Be kind, respectful, and patient with others.
- Assume good intent and give constructive feedback gracefully.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Focus on what's best for the community.

The following are not welcome here: harassment of any kind, personal or political attacks, insults,
publishing others' private information without permission, or any conduct that would reasonably be
considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope & enforcement

This Code applies in all community spaces and when representing the community in public.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please report it privately to the maintainers
at **[support@cloomba.com](mailto:support@cloomba.com)**. All reports will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly, and the maintainers will respect the privacy and security of the
reporter. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, and discussions that don't
align with this Code, and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviour they
deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
